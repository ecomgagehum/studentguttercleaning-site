    Why it matters in Austin

    Clogged gutters cost more than a cleaning.

    • Foundation. When gutters overflow, water dumps right next to the house. Austin's clay soil swells when wet and shrinks when dry, and that movement is how slab cracks start.
    • Fascia rot. Water that can't drain backs up behind the gutter and soaks the wood trim along your roofline.
    • Mosquitoes. Standing water in a clogged gutter is a breeding spot.

    When Austin trees fill your gutters

    TreeWhat dropsWhen
    PecanLeaves, husks, twigsMid to late November
    Cedar elm, red oakLeaves, acornsLate fall
    Live oakLeaves, then tasselsFebruary to April

    Under big oaks, most homes need a clean in late fall and again in spring after the tassels drop.

    Questions

    Do I need to be home?

    No. I just need access to the outside of the house. I'll text you before and after photos when I'm done.

    Do you clean gutters that have guards?

    Yes. Guards still clog, especially under oaks. It's $50 extra because each guard has to be cleaned off or lifted to get to the gutter underneath.

    Why is a Tuesday to Thursday booking cheaper?

    Those are my route days, when I'm already out working neighborhoods around Austin. Booking one of them saves me a trip, and I pass that on. If you need it done within 24 hours, I'll do that at the standard price.

    Do you install gutters or gutter guards?

    No. I only clean gutters and downspouts. That's the whole business.

    How often should gutters be cleaned in Austin?

    Under big trees, twice a year: late fall after the pecans and elms drop, and spring after the live oak tassels. With few trees, once a year is usually enough.
